## The setup

Dollar General spent the last two years cleaning up a mess of its own making: too much inventory, too few workers per store, and rising theft. The stock fell accordingly, and most analysts now treat it as a "show me" story.

## Where we differ

Consensus models a slow, linear margin recovery. We think recovery is **front-loaded**, because the remodel program targets the worst stores first. If even a third of those stores return to company-average margins, the earnings math changes meaningfully.

## What we're watching

The single most important number is **gross margin excluding shrink**. If it's rising while shrink is flat, our thesis is working.
