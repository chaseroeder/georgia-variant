## The setup

PayPal went from pandemic darling to value stock in under three years. The bear case is simple and widely held: everyone else has a better checkout button.

## Where we differ

The market is extrapolating the 2022–2023 share losses forward. We think the mix shift away from low-margin volume makes **profit** growth look much better than **volume** growth, and that's what drives the stock.
